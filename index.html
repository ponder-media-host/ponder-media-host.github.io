01 · ABOUT

A great product can only go so far without words people understand.

Ponder Media is run by Sarah Benali, a writer and marketer based in Spain. I work with fintech, payments and Web3 companies that have something complicated to explain, and turn it into content, copy and PR that people actually read.

My background is financial journalism: I wrote for Kitco News on gold, precious metals and markets, and I studied at IE Business School. That habit of getting to the point shapes everything I write.
